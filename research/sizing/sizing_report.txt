WHY THE BUCKET STRUCTURE IS NOT A SIGNAL
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A wider stop is further away, so it is hit less often — but the
  wins it produces are proportionally smaller. The two effects
  cancel, which is exactly what 'R already accounts for geometry'
  means. The numbers:
  bucket        stop rate   avg win R  avg loss R    exp R
  Q1 tightest       53.6%       1.628      -0.934   +0.028
  Q2                48.6%       1.298      -0.919   +0.019
  Q3                38.3%       1.238      -0.822   +0.156
  Q4                37.9%       1.087      -0.832   +0.065
  Q5 widest         35.9%       0.915      -0.801   +0.018

  Stop rate falls monotonically from tightest to widest, as geometry
  alone predicts. Expectancy does NOT — it is an inverted U with the
  middle bucket best, a shape with no mechanism behind it, and the
  walk-forward above shows it does not survive out of sample.
